Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

BioFlex collects minimal information necessary to provide our service. This includes your email address, chosen username, and any content you voluntarily upload to your profile.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate and improve BioFlex — displaying your public profile, tracking anonymous view counts, and providing account management. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Public Content

Your username, display name, bio, avatar, socials, and links are publicly visible on your profile page. Do not upload anything you wish to keep private.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal cookies for authentication and privacy-friendly analytics. No third-party advertising trackers are used.

5. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from your dashboard settings. All associated data will be permanently removed.

6. Content Guidelines

You may not use BioFlex to distribute illegal, harmful, or abusive content. Accounts violating these guidelines may be suspended or terminated.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of BioFlex after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at support@bioflex.cyou.
